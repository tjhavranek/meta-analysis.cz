A simulated expert panel that stress-tests and rebuilds your paper

10 June 2026  ·  Zuzana Irsova  ·  research tools / peer review

First published on LinkedIn, 10 June 2026. Archived with the rest of our writing at /komentare/posts/2026-06-10-a-panel-of-experts-for-your-paper/.

Imagine a panel of the world's leading experts, assembled for your research paper, arguing it out from rival schools and then revising it themselves.

Well, that's not possible. But we built an approximation with Claude Code, and it now uses the full power of Anthropic's Mythos-class model, Claude Fable 5.

Below is the skill, open and free. You give it your paper, ideally with the data and draft code. You get a stress test of your paper + a revision in track changes + a replication package. We ran it end to end on colleagues' papers and our own paper accepted at JPE Micro, Stata and R re-runs included. Enjoy:

https://github.com/tjhavranek/paper-workshop

meta-analysis.cz collects meta-analyses in economics: the papers, the data behind them, and the code that produced them. It is maintained by Tomas Havranek and Zuzana Irsova at the Institute of Economic Studies, Charles University, Prague.
