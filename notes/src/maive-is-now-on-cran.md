🔎 MAIVE is now on CRAN: Better tools for trustworthy meta-analysis

Meta-analysis is one of the key pillars of evidence-based research. It combines results from many studies to give us a clearer, more reliable answer.

But in observational research, studies can sometimes appear too precise --not because the evidence is strong, but because of method choices, selective reporting, or p-hacking. This can distort the final meta-analytic conclusion.
MAIVE helps address this.

You can now install MAIVE directly in R:

install.packages("MAIVE")

Or try it instantly in your browser (no coding needed):
🖥️ [https://www.easymeta.org](https://www.easymeta.org)
