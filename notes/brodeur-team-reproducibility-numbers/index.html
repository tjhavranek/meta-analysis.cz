A caveat on the Brodeur team's Nature reproducibility study

2 April 2026  ·  Zuzana Irsova  ·  economics / research tools

First published on LinkedIn, 2 April 2026. Archived with the rest of our writing at /komentare/posts/2026-04-02-brodeur-team-reproducibility-numbers/.

It was fun to be (a very small) part of the amazing team led by Abel Brodeur. The numbers look great, but note that we replicate papers in journals that have mandatory data and code sharing. So: share your data and code! And while you're at it, pre-register your papers and upload pre-analysis plans. This is the way!

meta-analysis.cz collects meta-analyses in economics: the papers, the data behind them, and the code that produced them. It is maintained by Tomas Havranek and Zuzana Irsova at the Institute of Economic Studies, Charles University, Prague.
