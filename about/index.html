Who maintains this site

Tomas Havranek, in Czech Tomáš Havránek, is at the Institute of Economic Studies, Faculty of Social Sciences, Charles University in Prague. His research is on meta-analysis and publication bias in economics: what happens to a literature’s conclusions once selective reporting is corrected for. ORCID: https://orcid.org/0000-0002-3158-2539.

Zuzana Irsova is at the same institute. Her full Slovak name is Zuzana Iršová Havránková. Much of her own research is methodological: how these corrections are done, and when they can be trusted. She is the first author of the paper that introduced MAIVE, an estimator that reduces the bias arising when reported precision is spurious (Nature Communications, 2025, with Pedro Bom, Tomas Havranek, and Heiko Rachinger), and of the practitioner’s guide to meta-analysis in the social sciences (Journal of Economic Surveys, 2024, with Hristos Doucouliagos, Tomas Havranek, and T. D. Stanley). ORCID: https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0753-8124.

The site collects the meta-analyses they have written with their co-authors, with the data and the estimation code behind them, and pools 40 of the 44 published datasets into the single table described on the datasets page. meta-analysis.cz collects meta-analyses in economics: the papers, the data behind them, and the code that produced them. It is maintained by Tomáš Havránek and Zuzana Iršová at the Institute of Economic Studies, Charles University, Prague.
