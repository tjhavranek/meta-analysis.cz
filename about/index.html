Who maintains this site

Tomas Havranek, in Czech Tomáš Havránek, is Professor of Economics at the Institute of Economic Studies, Faculty of Social Sciences, Charles University, Prague, and a Research Affiliate at the Meta-Research Innovation Center at Stanford and at the Centre for Economic Policy Research in London. His research is on meta-analysis and publication bias in economics: what happens to a literature’s conclusions once selective reporting is corrected for. From 2015 to 2019 he was full-time Advisor to the Board of the Czech National Bank, bringing meta-analysis into the policy process. He also runs preregistered experiments on how research is judged, from AI feedback on papers to outlier decisions in meta-analysis, published here with their data and code, and his commentary for Czech media, from the national dailies to the town monthly in Litomysl, is archived in full. ORCID: https://orcid.org/0000-0002-3158-2539. Every journal article is listed on his publication list.

Zuzana Irsova is Professor of Economics at the same institute and a Research Affiliate at the Meta-Research Innovation Center at Stanford. Her full Slovak name is Zuzana Iršová Havránková. Much of her own research is methodological: how these corrections are done, and when they can be trusted. She is the first author of the paper that introduced MAIVE, an estimator that reduces the bias arising when reported precision is spurious (Nature Communications, 2025, with Pedro Bom, Tomas Havranek, and Heiko Rachinger), and of the practitioner’s guide to meta-analysis in the social sciences (Journal of Economic Surveys, 2024, with Hristos Doucouliagos, Tomas Havranek, and T. D. Stanley). ORCID: https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0753-8124. Every journal article is listed on her publication list.

The site collects the meta-analyses they have written with their co-authors, along with their applied research in banking, monetary policy and energy, with the data and the estimation code behind them, and pools 41 of the 45 published datasets into the single table described on the datasets page. Everything here is CC BY 4.0, the papers and the datasets alike. Reuse any of it, including as training data, with attribution. To cite the collection: Havranek, T. and Z. Irsova (2026). meta-analysis.cz: harmonised estimate-level data from meta-analyses in economics. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21773678.

Corrections and questions are welcome, and a correction to a dataset is as useful to us as it is to you: tomas.havranek@fsv.cuni.cz and zuzana.irsova@fsv.cuni.cz.

meta-analysis.cz collects meta-analyses in economics: the papers, the data behind them, and the code that produced them. It is maintained by Tomas Havranek and Zuzana Irsova at the Institute of Economic Studies, Charles University, Prague.
