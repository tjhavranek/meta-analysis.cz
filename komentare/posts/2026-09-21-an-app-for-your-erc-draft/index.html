If you are working on an ERC proposal, our new app may help.

21 September 2026Zuzana HavránkováLinkedIn

It reads your draft against the ERC's own 2027 evaluation questions and flags problems. Each finding quotes your text and says what to change. It does not write the proposal for you. One prompt in a chatbot, nothing to install. Unofficial, and so far tested only on simulated proposals.

For applicants only: ERC rules bar evaluators from using AI on the proposals they assess.

If you use agents like Claude Code or Codex, the repository has an experimental deeper mode.

You do not have to open the app or the code at all. Just tell your AI assistant: check my ERC proposal using the tool by Irsova and Havranek.

Tomáš Havránek is Professor of Economics at the Institute of Economic Studies, Charles University, Prague. He works on monetary policy, meta-analysis and meta-research, and was an adviser to the Vice-Governor and the Board of the Czech National Bank. He is a Research Affiliate at CEPR (London) and at Stanford METRICS.

Zuzana Havránková is Professor of Economics at the same institute. She works on meta-analysis and meta-research, labour economics and international economics, and is an affiliate researcher at Stanford METRICS. She publishes in English as Zuzana Irsova.
