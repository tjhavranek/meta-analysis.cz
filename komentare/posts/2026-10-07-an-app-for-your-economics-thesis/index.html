If you are writing a bachelor's or master's thesis in economics, our new app can help.

7 October 2026Zuzana HavránkováLinkedIn

It reads your idea, proposal, or draft the way your opponent will. It quotes your text and says what to change. It does not write the thesis for you.

One prompt in ChatGPT or Claude, nothing to install. Built for the thesis seminars at IES, Charles University. Unofficial and free.

If you use Claude Code or Codex, the repository has a deeper mode that also checks your code.

Or just tell your AI assistant: check with the thesis tool from meta-analysis.cz.

Tomas Havranek is Professor of Economics at the Institute of Economic Studies, Charles University, Prague. He works on monetary policy, meta-analysis and meta-research, and was an adviser to the Vice-Governor and the Board of the Czech National Bank. He is a Research Affiliate at CEPR (London) and at Stanford METRICS.

Zuzana Irsova is Professor of Economics at the same institute. She works on meta-analysis and meta-research, labour economics and international economics, and is an affiliate researcher at Stanford METRICS. She writes in Czech as Zuzana Havránková.
