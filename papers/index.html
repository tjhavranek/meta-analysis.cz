All 66 papers on this site are here in full, as HTML rather than PDF. Each keeps its PDF and, where the paper has one, its link to the version of record. 56 are meta-analyses and meta-research, each answering a question on Headline results. The other 10 carry no headline result of their own: the two 2026 MAER-Net notes on AI, a mass reproduction of 110 published studies, and applied work in banking, monetary policy and energy.

Figures are reproduced wherever the artwork lifted cleanly off the page — 264 so far. Where it did not, the caption stands on its own and the PDF has the picture.

meta-analysis.cz collects meta-analyses in economics: the papers, the data behind them, and the code that produced them. It is maintained by Tomas Havranek and Zuzana Irsova at the Institute of Economic Studies, Charles University, Prague.
