54 of the papers on this site are republished here in full, as HTML rather than as a PDF: the whole text, the tables as tables, the mathematics as mathematics, and the references as links. They read on a phone, they are searchable, a screen reader can read them aloud, and a language model can quote them without guessing at a scanned column. Each one also keeps its PDF and its link to the version of record.

Figures are reproduced where the artwork could be lifted cleanly from the page — 226 of them so far. Where it could not, the figure’s caption is printed on its own and the PDF has the picture. A caption standing alone is a gap, not a claim that the paper has no figure there.

meta-analysis.cz collects meta-analyses in economics: the papers, the data behind them, and the code that produced them. It is maintained by Tomas Havranek and Zuzana Irsova at the Institute of Economic Studies, Charles University, Prague.
