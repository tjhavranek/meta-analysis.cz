Type a question and an AI answers from the papers, notes, and datasets on this site, with numbered links to the sources. It says so when the site does not cover the question.

An answer takes about 10 seconds.

Answers are not a personal statement by the authors. This is a pilot on a free service, so it gives about 50 answers a day in total.

We keep questions and answers for 30 days so that a wrong answer can be corrected or withdrawn; nothing else about you is stored. They are processed by Cloudflare, which also protects the page from bots (Turnstile). Questions from one address are counted only within the day, under an unreadable code, and wiped every night.

meta-analysis.cz collects meta-analyses, the data behind them, the code that produced them, and the methods for doing them. It is maintained by Tomas Havranek and Zuzana Irsova at the Institute of Economic Studies, Charles University, Prague.
